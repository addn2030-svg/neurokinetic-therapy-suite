## 🧠 What is NeuroKinetic Therapy (NKT®)?

NeuroKinetic Therapy is a sophisticated system of neuromuscular assessment and corrective movement developed by David Weinstock. Based on the neurobiology of **Motor Control Theory**, NKT identifies the root cause of chronic pain, postural distortion, and athletic movement dysfunction by evaluating the **Motor Control Center (MCC)** located in the cerebellum.

### The Central Diagnostic Algorithm:
```
[ Step 1: Test Baseline Muscle ]      ---> Identifies functionally INHIBITED receiver (Spongy give).
               |
               v
[ Step 2: Challenge / TL Driver ]     ---> Sensory stimulation (rub/touch) to suspected culprit.
               |
               v
[ Step 3: Immediate Retest (< 2s) ]   ---> Muscle shifts from Weak to ROCK SOLID STRONG.
               |                           (Reactive Pair Confirmed!)
               v
[ Step 4: Release Facilitated Driver] ---> 30–60s manual release, ischemic compression, or IASTM.
               |
               v
[ Step 5: Activate Inhibited Muscle ] ---> Immediate isolated isometric contraction (5s x 4 reps)
               |                           within the 30-60s cerebellar neuroplastic window.
               v
[ Step 6: Sequential Daily Homework ] ---> RULE: 1. Release Driver FIRST --> 2. Activate Victim SECOND.
```

## 📦 Curriculum Overview

### Level 1: Foundations of Motor Control & Manual Muscle Testing
* Hierarchical motor control (Cerebral Cortex $\rightarrow$ Limbic System $\rightarrow$ Cerebellum $\rightarrow$ Basal Ganglia $\rightarrow$ Spinal Cord).
* Neurological binary MMT (rate coding and recruitment latency) vs. Orthopedic Kendall grading (0–5 scale).
* Synergistic Dominance (TFL $\rightarrow$ Glute Med; Hamstrings $\rightarrow$ Glute Max; Upper Trap $\rightarrow$ Serratus).
* Reciprocal Inhibition (Psoas $\rightarrow$ Glute Max; Pec Minor $\rightarrow$ Lower Trap; Calves $\rightarrow$ Tibialis Anterior).

### Level 2: Functional Whole-Body Subsystems, Core & Cranial Systems
* 4 Kinetic Subsystems: Posterior Oblique (POS), Anterior Oblique (AOS), Deep Longitudinal (DLS), Lateral Subsystem.
* Core Cylinder & Respiratory Diaphragm Assessment (Inhalation vs. Exhalation breath-hold tests).
* TMJ / Stomatognathic System (Masseter, Temporalis, Medial/Lateral Pterygoids, Digastric).
* Perturbation & Anticipatory Postural Adjustments (APAs).
* Joint Capsule Receptors (Joint Compression vs. Decompression Reflexes).

### Level 3: Fine Somatosensory Integration, Scars, Ligaments & Cranial Reflexes
* Multi-Phasic Advanced Gait Dynamics (Stance, Swing, Propulsion, Cross-Body Coupling).
* 8-Directional Multi-Vector Scar Protocol (Surgical, Traumatic, Navel, Episiotomy, Scar-to-Scar).
* Ligamentous Interlinks & Reflexes (ATFL, CFL, Sacrotuberous, Iliolumbar, SIJ, Alar).
* Extraocular Muscles & Oculo-Cervical Reflexes (CN III, IV, VI Gaze Vectors).
* Hyoids, Deglutition & Tongue Stabilizers.
* Reverse Therapy Localization (Reverse TL) & Layered Onion Unraveling.

## 🛠️ Clinical Tools & Adjunctive Equipment Guide
* **Kinesiology Taping (RockTape):** Directional taping for facilitation (Origin to Insertion, 25-50% tension), inhibition (Insertion to Origin, 0-15% tension), and scar decompression.
* **IASTM Tools:** Stainless steel bevel tools for 30–60 second mechanoreceptive stimulation.
* **Sensory Tuning Forks (128 Hz):** Pacinian corpuscle stimulation for ligamentous rehabilitation.
* **Assessment Sheets:** Printable intake forms, 10-point neurological screening, and reactive pair diagnostic logs.
