Master Clinical Demonstration 2: Frame-by-Frame 4-Step NKT Corrective Sequence
Step-by-Step Protocol Sequence

Clinical Focus: The full end-to-end corrective sequence demonstrated on a patient with chronic anterior shoulder impingement driven by a hypertonic Pectoralis Minor reciprocally suppressing the Middle and Lower Trapezius.

Panel 1 Step 1: Baseline Neurological MMT: Middle Trapezius is isolated in prone/supine position. The muscle exhibits delayed recruitment latency and spongy collapse under light-to-moderate isometric load.

Panel 2 Step 2: Therapy Localization / Tactile Challenge: Patient touches the ipsilateral Pectoralis Minor belly (or the clinician briskly rubs the origin at ribs 3–5). Retesting the Middle Trapezius immediately turns it rock solid strong (Reactive Pair confirmed).

Panel 3 Step 3: 45-Second Manual Myofascial Release: The clinician performs targeted deep tissue ischemic compression / pin-and-stretch to the Pectoralis Minor for 45 seconds to reset muscle spindle sensitivity.

Panel 4 Step 4: Isolated Isometric Activation: Without delay, the patient performs 4 repetitions of a 5-second isometric Middle Trapezius retraction hold to burn the restored motor engram into the cerebellum.
